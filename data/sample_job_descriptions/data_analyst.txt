Job Title: Data Analyst

We are hiring a Data Analyst to help turn raw data into actionable business insights.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, IT, or a related quantitative field.
- Strong proficiency in SQL for data extraction and analysis.
- Working knowledge of Python (Pandas, NumPy) for data manipulation.
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Understanding of statistics and data analysis fundamentals.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Preferred:
- Experience with Excel for reporting and dashboards.
- Basic understanding of Machine Learning concepts.
- Familiarity with data visualization libraries such as Matplotlib or Seaborn.

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean, and analyze large datasets from multiple sources.
- Build dashboards and reports to communicate insights to stakeholders.
- Identify trends and patterns to support data-driven decision making.
- Work closely with business teams to understand analytical requirements.
